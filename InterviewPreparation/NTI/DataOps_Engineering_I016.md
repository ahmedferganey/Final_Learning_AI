## Part 1: Technical Program - Duration: 294 Hours.

### Objectives:

- This track aims to equip trainees with the knowledge and practical skills required for real-world careers in Data Engineering, ETL Development, Data Pipeline Engineering, Cloud Data Platforms, Data Automation, and Big Data Systems. Participants will learn how to design, build, automate, and manage scalable data pipelines and modern data infrastructures, enabling organizations to efficiently collect, process, store, and analyze large volumes of data to support data-driven decision-making and digital transformation initiatives.

### Who May Apply?

- Graduates of:
  - Engineering or Science (Computer Science, Statistics and Mathematics).
  - Computing and Informatics.
  - Computing and Artificial Intelligence.
  - Computing and Data Science.

### Job Profile:

- **Data Engineer:** Designs, builds, and maintains scalable data pipelines and data infrastructure.
- **ETL Developer:** Develops and manages ETL processes for extracting, transforming, and loading data from multiple sources.
- **Big Data Engineer:** Implements and manages large-scale data processing systems using big data technologies.
- **Cloud Data Engineer:** Designs and deploys data solutions on cloud platforms, ensuring scalability, security, and performance.
- **DataOps Engineer:** Automates data workflows, monitors data pipelines, and improves the reliability and efficiency of data operations.

## Part 2: Career development - Duration: 126 Hours.

### Overview:

- This comprehensive course is designed for aspiring professionals in the ICT field who aim to excel in international business environments. Participants will develop proficiency in Business English, enabling them to confidently communicate and present in professional settings. Through hands-on simulations and workshops, attendees will practice leadership and team-building skills, fostering autonomy and critical thinking.
- The course also covers essential soft skills, including effective communication, time management, and problem-solving. Additionally, participants will gain a solid foundation in project management, equipping them with the tools to manage projects efficiently across various industries, such as Construction, Information Technology, and more.
- A new focus on essential workplace skills will prepare participants to thrive in a corporate environment, covering key areas such as teamwork, conflict resolution, and professional ethics.
