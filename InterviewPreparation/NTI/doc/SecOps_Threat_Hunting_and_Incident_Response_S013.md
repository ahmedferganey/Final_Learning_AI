# SecOps: Threat Hunting and Incident Response (S013)

![SecOps: Threat Hunting and Incident Response](https://nti.sci.eg/dey/images/tracks/S013.jpg)

## Part 1: Technical Program (294 Hours)

### Overview

- This course provides a comprehensive, hands-on foundation in modern Security Operations Center (SOC) environments, blending essential technical skills with advanced defensive strategies. Participants begin by mastering critical infrastructure components—such as IP networking, Linux system administration, and foundational programming using Python, PowerShell, and Bash—tailored specifically for day-to-day SOC operations. Building upon this technical base, students explore core security monitoring through Cisco CyberOps Associate training while gaining practical proficiency with industry-leading analytical platforms like FortiSIEM and FortiAnalyzer to detect, triage, and analyze live network events.
- The second half of the program transitions into proactive defense, threat intelligence, and structured incident management workflows central to any high-performing SOC. Learners delve into Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) frameworks, such as MITRE ATT&CK and the Cyber Kill Chain, before advancing to sophisticated threat hunting methodologies, adversary emulation, and detection engineering using Sigma rules. Finally, the curriculum covers the complete incident response lifecycle, escalation protocols, and introductory digital forensics, equipping analysts with the vital skills needed to reduce dwell times, investigate complex intrusions, preserve evidence integrity, and successfully coordinate remediation efforts within enterprise security operations.

- **Lab Environment Construction:** Build a multi-VM lab including Domain Controller, Windows Clients, Firewall (pfSense), and Log Aggregation Platform (ELK/HELK) on VMware ESXi.
- **Tool Configuration & Deployment:** Install, configure, and manage security/logging tools like Sysmon, Winlogbeat, and CALDERA agents.

## Part 2: Career Development (126 Hours)

### Overview

- This comprehensive course is designed for aspiring professionals in the ICT field who aim to excel in international business environments. Participants will develop proficiency in Business English, enabling them to confidently communicate and present in professional settings. Through hands-on simulations and workshops, attendees will practice leadership and team-building skills, fostering autonomy and critical thinking.
- The course also covers essential soft skills, including effective communication, time management, and problem-solving. Additionally, participants will gain a solid foundation in project management, equipping them with the tools to manage projects efficiently across various industries, such as Construction, Information Technology, and more.
- A new focus on essential workplace skills will prepare participants to thrive in a corporate environment, covering key areas such as teamwork, conflict resolution, and professional ethics.
