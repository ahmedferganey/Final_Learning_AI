## 1. Internet of Things (IoT)

### Module Information

- **Module name:** Internet of Things (IoT)
- **Module level:** 1
- **Technical part duration:** 60 hours

### Module Description

In this course, trainees learn how IoT solutions transform the physical world into a complex, dynamic system of connected devices at an unprecedented scale. IoT is a major 21st-century technology, with a wide range of products, tools, applications, and career opportunities.

By the end of the module, trainees are expected to gain the IoT knowledge needed to pursue opportunities in IoT companies.

Trainees study using official **Cisco Networking Academy** materials and take online academy exams. The program states that trainees may acquire a **100% discount voucher** for the international certification exam.

### Target Audience

- Communications and electronics engineers
- Computer engineers
- Mechatronics engineers
- Biomedical engineers
- Computer and information sciences graduates
- Graduates of other university disciplines

### Prerequisites

- Basic programming principles
- Basic understanding of sensors and microcontroller concepts

### Module Contents

1. Introduction to the Internet of Things (IoT)
2. IoT architectures and protocols
3. Introduction to C programming
4. Interfacing sensors and actuators with Raspberry Pi to implement IoT
5. Implementing an IoT broker and clients
6. Cisco Packet Tracer: sensors and the Packet Tracer microcontroller
7. Sensor and actuator interfacing for IoT
8. Fog and cloud computing
9. Developing IoT applications
10. Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT)
11. Implementing an IoT platform based on MQTT
12. IoT-based projects, such as:
    - Smart city
    - Smart home
    - Smart factory
    - IoT-based e-health
